# Current Thesis

## T1 — View-Based World Model

**Claim**

Existing World Model research can be re-described as models/views over a world rather than as the world itself, and the distinction

`Physical → Interface → Cognition → Data → Symbolic`

may expose meaningful differences that conventional World Model taxonomies obscure.

**Status:** proposed / unverified

**Confidence:** low

## Antithesis A1

The five-layer order adds little explanatory value because existing World Model formalisms already capture observation, latent state, data, prediction, and action sufficiently well.

## Falsification conditions

T1 should be weakened or rejected if systematic comparison shows that:

1. the five layers collapse into existing standard categories without additional explanatory power;
2. the order cannot distinguish important World Model families;
3. view boundaries cannot be represented consistently;
4. the ontology introduces more ambiguity than it resolves;
5. experiments produce no measurable improvement in comparison, explanation, or interoperability.

## Revision rule

A thesis may be:

- strengthened by convergent evidence,
- qualified by boundary conditions,
- split into narrower claims,
- or rejected by counterevidence.

Never overwrite historical claims. Record revisions as dated research evidence.
